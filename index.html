How is SHR calculated?

SHR = admission glucose (mg/dL) ÷ [28.7 × HbA1c (%) − 46.7].

If glucose is measured in mmol/L, convert it to mg/dL using the study convention first. All measurements must be available by the prediction time.

MODEL NOTESModel & validation details＋

Model version: 20261113. Risk is averaged across 20 locked XGBoost models, each with 100 trees. No training or parameter updates occur in the browser.

Explicit missingness is supported for age, bicarbonate, respiratory rate, white blood cell count, blood urea nitrogen, and mean arterial pressure. Stroke and atrial fibrillation remain required. The locked imputer does not support missing SHR. The separate, explicitly selected SHR option substitutes the training-set median supplied by the project owner (1.088619) before running the locked model. It is a scenario estimate, not a validated missing-SHR prediction method. If other predictors are missing, their locked imputation also uses the substituted SHR. Adult eligibility must be confirmed even when exact age is unavailable. The frozen, outcome-free FCS-PMM training imputer uses 20 imputations, 10 iterations, and 5 candidate donors, with a fixed single-case seed of 20261113. Identical inputs produce identical results. Single-case imputation draws may differ from the original study’s batch predictions.

The source materials report an internal-test AUC of 0.7758 and an external AUC of 0.7937. Development data use minimum WBC, BUN, and bicarbonate values; external data use first-24-hour means. SHR aggregation also differs. These performance estimates do not validate the training-median SHR scenario. They come from the source study; numerical checks of this browser implementation do not constitute new clinical validation.

The SHR median was supplied by the project owner from the figure caption “Dotted vertical lines mark median SHR 1.088619;”. The training data were not available for independent verification of this statistic.

The reference synthetic case has a risk of 3.4412096% with complete inputs and 4.4328317% when only bicarbonate is missing. View standard-mode numerical verification report ↗ · View median-SHR numerical verification report ↗
